# Community and Scope Boundaries

## Creative scope

Support recognizable TripleT images, short scenes, bumpers, fake ads, game-world remixes, and image-to-video packaging. Community creations are unofficial by default.

Separate:

1. **Approved visual identity** — the bundled TripleT turnaround
2. **Verified community context** — supplied or community-linked material
3. **Historical/rendering grounding** — inspected original-platform game references
4. **Creative interpretation** — the new scene or invented premise

Do not present a creative interpretation as official lore, endorsement, or partnership.

## Token boundary

This is a creative engine, not a trading engine. Do not add token charts, candlesticks, price calls, profit promises, countdown pressure, contract addresses, or spam-style ticker repetition unless the user explicitly needs a safe piece of factual design copy. Never provide financial advice, market manipulation, guaranteed returns, or unsupported investment claims.

## Cultural care

Do not invent claims about the origin, meaning, or ownership of TripleT / Tung Tung Tung Sahur. When a request depends on cultural or religious context, verify the context from credible sources, distinguish fact from internet remix, and avoid using a community joke to demean a real practice or group.

## Attribution

When helpful, use neutral language:

> Community-created TripleT scene using the TripleT Community Engine by Nosimaj Media.

Do not claim that Nosimaj Media owns the underlying meme identity or that an output is officially endorsed without evidence.

## General safety

Follow the platform's normal safety rules. For risky real-person, hateful, sexual, illegal, or deceptive requests, preserve the safe creative portion when possible without weakening ordinary harmless absurdity.
