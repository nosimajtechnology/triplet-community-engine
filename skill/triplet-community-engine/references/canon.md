# TripleT Identity Canon

## Authority

The bundled [character sheet](../assets/triplet-character-sheet.jpg) is the approved Tier 1 authority for TripleT's PS2 character model. It includes front, three-quarter, side, and rear views plus the separate wooden club. Use the full turnaround to preserve construction rather than copying only the front pose.

Supporting official or community-linked references may clarify meme context, but they do not override the approved PS2 model unless the user explicitly assigns them that role.

## Immutable identity traits

- one upright, elongated rectangular wooden body with a gently rounded flat top
- tall narrow silhouette; the torso is the main trunk rather than a human body inside a costume
- warm medium-brown wood with visible vertical grain running through the body and limbs
- two very large round eyes with pale sclera, dark pupils, and dark rims
- thick dark arched eyebrows above the eyes
- long narrow protruding nose with a clear side-profile point
- small closed smile and calm, uncanny default expression
- extremely thin wooden arms with small readable hands
- two long thin wooden legs emerging separately from the bottom of the body
- oversized bare feet with individually readable toes
- no clothing by default
- low-poly, low-resolution, slightly rough PS2 in-engine construction

## Primary prop

The wooden club is a separate tapered object: thick rounded striking end, long narrowing shaft, and small grip end. Preserve its shape when used. It may be carried, planted, dropped, leaned, or omitted according to the scene.

The club must never become a tail, nose, extra limb, body extension, duplicate prop, or permanently fused accessory.

## Controlled variables

Change when the user or approved scene requires:

- pose, action, expression, and eye direction
- clothing, uniforms, hats, or scene-specific accessories
- whether the club is present and its current condition
- location, time, weather, lighting, and props
- aspect ratio, camera, and named PS2 game grammar
- sincere, deadpan, eerie, absurd, or commercial tone

Clothing must fit around the established rectangular body instead of reshaping it into human anatomy.

## Forbidden drift

- generic log monster, tree creature, plank mascot, stick figure, or human in a wooden suit
- rounded cylinder, square cube, short block, tapered torso, bark-covered trunk, or branch-like silhouette
- missing or tiny eyes, cheerful cartoon pupils, absent brows, short nose, open mouth, or large toothy grin
- muscular human arms or legs, short thick limbs, shoes replacing the bare feet without request, or missing toes
- horizontal or noisy bark texture replacing the clean vertical grain
- glossy plastic, polished Pixar-like CGI, current-gen realism, photoreal wood sculpture, or mobile-game rendering
- a club fused to the body, duplicated, or carried when the approved project state says it is absent
- multiple TripleTs unless requested
- TripleT traits leaking into secondary characters

## Reference assignment block

Use this internally:

```text
TRIPLET IDENTITY AUTHORITY — bundled turnaround; body, face, limbs, feet, grain, silhouette.
PROJECT AUTHORITY — latest approved frame; wardrobe, props, environment, light, geography, action.
COMMUNITY CONTEXT — verified source; meme premise or current community cue only.
GAMEPLAY REFERENCES — rendering, environment density, camera, and animation only.
SECONDARY IDENTITIES — one separate reference block per character.
```

Identity preservation outranks decorative style. Simplify background density, effects, motion, or shot count before relaxing the protected construction.
